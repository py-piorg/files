Safety first

Simple habits make LPG safer.

Keep this guidance close. When in doubt, turn off the supply, move to fresh air, and contact the station team.

At home

Four essentials to remember.

01 / STORE

Keep it upright

Store cylinders upright in a cool, well-ventilated place away from direct heat, flames, and ignition sources.

02 / CHECK

Inspect the connection

Before use, check the hose, regulator, seal, and valve for damage or signs of wear.

03 / VENTILATE

Give gas room

Never use LPG in a sealed or poorly ventilated space. Keep air moving around the appliance.

04 / RESPOND

Act on a smell

If you smell gas, avoid flames and electrical switches, close the valve if safe, and leave the area.

If you smell gas

Move calmly. Make the area safe.

  • Turn off the cylinder valve if you can do so safely.
  • Open doors and windows to ventilate.
  • Do not light a match or operate electrical switches.
  • Move outside and call Triple A Gas or emergency services.
