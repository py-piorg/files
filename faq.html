Safety
Follow these steps immediately:
  1. Turn off the cylinder valve
  2. Open windows and doors to ventilate the area
  3. Do not switch any electrical devices on or off
  4. Do not light matches or use lighters
  5. Leave the area and call 024 507 5496 immediately
For more safety tips, visit our Safety page.
Keep the cylinder upright at all times, in a well-ventilated area away from heat sources, direct sunlight, and flammable materials. Never store cylinders indoors inside closed cupboards. Ensure the valve is fully closed when not in use.
Before every refill, check:
  • The hose for cracks, kinks, or signs of wear
  • The regulator is seated correctly and seals tightly
  • The valve opens and closes smoothly
  • Apply soapy water to connections — bubbles mean a leak
If you notice any damage, bring the cylinder to your nearest Triple A Gas outlet for inspection.
