About Triple A Gas

The reliable energy partner behind everyday life.

For more than two decades, we have helped households, drivers, and businesses access LPG with clarity, care, and consistency.

Our story

Built around trust, not just supply.

Triple A Gas is a licensed LPG marketing company established in 2005. From our Accra head office to our stations in Antoa, Bonwire, Bogoso, and Prestea, our focus is simple: make gas easy to buy and safe to use.

We combine dependable refills, cylinder exchange, vehicle gas refueling, and bulk supply with practical guidance for the people and businesses we serve.

  • Transparent, per-kilogram refills
  • Five growing station locations
  • Trained teams and safety-led operations
