Public company profile

Triple A Gas Company Limited in Ghana

COMAC Ghana's member directory lists Triple A LP Gas Limited. A Global LPG Partnership report, using 2017 sector data, records the company as an LPG-only seller with 1,599.1 metric tonnes of LPG sales and a 0.6% market share for that year.

Publicly listed location A public map listing places Triple A Gas in Ga East Municipal Assembly, Greater Accra. Availability, hours and services can change, so please confirm directly before visiting.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

From a vision to a trusted LPG supplier serving households and commercial clients across four regions of Ghana.

2005

Company Founded

Triple A Gas Company Limited is established as an Oil Marketing Company specialising in LPG, registered with the Registrar General's Department (Registration No. CA-18,223).

2005–2010

Initial Growth Phase

Established the flagship retail outlet in Agbogba, Greater Accra. Built lasting relationships with household customers and small commercial kitchens across the municipality.

2011–2017

Regional Expansion

Expanded operations to Central, Ashanti, and Western regions. Added retail outlets in Apam, Antoa, Bonwire, Bogoso, Prestea, and Klagon. Achieved 1,599.1 MT in annual LPG sales by 2017.

2017–Present

Industry Recognition & Continued Growth

Listed in the COMAC Ghana member directory. Continues to serve thousands of households, eateries, and industrial clients across 7 retail outlets in 4 regions with safety-first LPG delivery.
